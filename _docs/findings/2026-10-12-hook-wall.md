# The hook wall: hanging loops, tags, gestures, and wall links (2026-10-12)

## Question

Replace the velvet cones with a display where every bracelet faces the viewer and reads, on a phone, with room to grow.

## What was rejected and why

- Cones (shipped 2026-10-09 to 10-12): the display piece was the hero; bracelets were small, stacked at the bottom third of a cone, and half of every phrase sat around the back. Spinning to read is a chore.
- Shadow box and tiered acrylic stand: offered, not chosen. Both are geometric and would render, but hooks read best and scale as a grid.
- Tags beside the peg (first build): clipped at the canvas edge on the last column; mirroring them to the left crossed the neighbor's loop. Inside the loop, hanging straight down from the peg, they sit in the bracelet's bare interior and never cross anything.
- Fixed tap-to-jingle plus hold-to-open: hold alone was undiscoverable (session 5 review). Now tap opens the options (with a lift and jingle); hold then drag moves a bracelet; a plain drag scrolls.

## How the loop is drawn (`loopPath`, `buildRing` in `src/main.js`)

- The loop is a closed pear: `x = -sin(t) * (1 - 0.38 cos(t))`, `y = -1.15 R (1 - cos t)`, scaled so the half-width is R. Traced from the peg down the left side, along the bottom left to right, and back up, so beads along the bottom read in order. Beads are placed by arc length with `pointAt`, the middle of the bead run at the bottom, and rotated to the tangent (`rotation.z = p.a`; hanging charms counter-rotate so they hang down).
- Bead size is `min(grid.S, 0.9 * perimeter / U)`: a full bracelet fills the loop; a short one shows cord. R comes from the column width (`wallGrid`: 2 columns under 520px, 3 under 760, 4 above, `R = 0.34 * colW` clamped 34 to 92).
- The group's origin is the point of the cord on the peg, so a swing is `rotation.z` about the peg. Pendulum: `av += ((rest - a) * 40 - av * 1.7) * dt`, with `rest = tiltS * 0.35`.
- Two strands: both cords run around the loop offset along the normal by `cordGap`, meeting at shared beads and at the peg.

## Tags (`tagTexture`, `makeTag`)

A 320 x 196 canvas: kraft (or blush for trades) rounded card, grain, an eyelet punched with `destination-out` at the top center, name shrunk to fit then ellipsised, date under it, a rotated red "TRADED" stamp for trades. Plane width `1.18 R` (58 to 104 px), hung at `y = -0.66 R - h/2` on a thread from the peg. `MeshStandardMaterial` so it dims at night.

## Gestures and scrolling (pointer handlers, `updateStack`)

- `spin.ring` is the bracelet under the press. A 300ms hold sets `spin.lift`; moves then set `r.drag` and `previewOrder` reorders `rings` to the slot under the pointer (`slotAt`), the others gliding to new pegs. Release writes `stack` from `rings` and saves.
- The room (`room` group: sign and rails) scrolls by `scrollY`, from drag (`spin.scrolling`), wheel, or keyboard focus (`scrollToRing`). `scrollMax` is `wallGrid().total - H`.
- Entries are objects `{ b, n, d, t }`; `entryOf` upgrades bare bead lists on load. `saveStack` is a no-op while visiting.

## Wall links (`wallLink`, `readVisit`)

`#w=` then entries `code,name,YYYYMMDD,t` joined by `;`, then `c` (wall color), `k` (sign color), `s` (sign word), `u` (name), `l=0` / `x=0` (props off). About 80 characters per bracelet; 24 bracelets is about 2 KB, fine for a link, too long for a QR code. The visitor's stack and decor are held in memory and restored by `endVisit`; `setMode` ends a visit when leaving the wall. "Take a copy" must call `setMode('line')` before `receiveTrade`, because the trade stringing only adds beads while stringing.

## Installable

`public/manifest.webmanifest`, `public/sw.js` (navigations network first with cache fallback, everything else cache first, Google Fonts kept once seen, old caches dropped by `CACHE` name), registered only on https. Icons were drawn with Python PIL from the favicon design; the script was not kept, so redraw from the favicon SVG if the icons need changing.
